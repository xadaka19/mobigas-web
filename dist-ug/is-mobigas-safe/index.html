Is MobiGas Safe and Legitimate?

If you're new to gas-on-credit, it's reasonable to ask how it works and whether it's trustworthy. Here's exactly how MobiGas operates, in plain terms.

MobiGas is a marketplace, not a lender

We never lend you money or hold your funds. All credit is provided exclusively by regulated partner banks and SACCOs licensed by the Central Bank of Kenya.

Every vendor is verified

Gas vendors on the platform go through a verification process including business registration and ID checks before they can receive orders.

Your credit is assessed by a real bank

Your credit limit comes from an instant CRB (Credit Reference Bureau) check run by our partner bank — the same kind of check used for formal bank loans.

Your data is protected

All personal data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and handled according to Kenya's Data Protection Act (KDPA). We never sell your data.

What MobiGas does NOT do

• We do not lend money or charge interest ourselves \u2014 that's set by your partner bank

• We do not hold your gas, money, or any physical inventory

• We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties

• We do not make credit approval decisions \u2014 the bank does, based on your CRB check
