Cooking Gas Safety Tips in Kenya

LPG cooking gas is safe when handled correctly, but improper use can lead to dangerous leaks and fires. Here are essential safety tips for every Kenyan household using cooking gas — whether you order through MobiGas or buy gas any other way.

Safe gas cylinder handling

Always store cylinders upright in a well-ventilated area outside the kitchen
Never store cylinders near heat sources, open flames, or direct sunlight
Check the expiry date on your cylinder — expired cylinders should not be used
Only use cylinders, regulators, and pipes from reputable suppliers
Inspect the rubber hose and regulator regularly for cracks or wear

What to do if you smell gas

Do not switch any lights or electrical appliances on or off
Extinguish all open flames immediately — candles, matches, cigarettes
Open all windows and doors to ventilate the space
Turn off the gas cylinder valve at the source
Leave the building and contact your gas supplier or emergency services
Do not re-enter until the area is fully ventilated and the leak is fixed

Safe practices when cooking

Never leave a lit gas cooker unattended for extended periods
Turn off the cylinder valve when not cooking, not just the cooker knob
Keep flammable materials such as paper, cloth, and plastic away from the flame
Ensure your cooker and cylinder are on a stable, level surface
If a burner fails to light, turn off and ventilate before trying again
